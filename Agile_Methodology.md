# Agile Methodology

Agile is a software development approach in which software is developed and delivered in small, incremental steps instead of building the entire application at once.

It focuses on:

- Continuous development and delivery
- Regular customer feedback
- Team collaboration
- Adapting to changing requirements
- Continuous testing and improvement

## 1. How Does Agile Work?

Agile development is divided into small iterations called **sprints**. A sprint usually lasts one or two weeks, though its length can vary depending on the team.

### Agile Sprint Cycle

1. **Product Backlog**  
   A prioritized list of project requirements and tasks.

2. **Sprint Planning**  
   The team selects tasks from the backlog to complete during the sprint.

3. **Development**  
   The team develops the selected features, including frontend and backend work.

4. **Testing**  
   The team tests the features and fixes bugs.

5. **Sprint Review**  
   The team demonstrates the completed features and gathers feedback.

6. **Sprint Retrospective**  
   The team discusses what went well and identifies improvements for the next sprint.

**Next sprint → Repeat the cycle**

## 2. Agile Methodology: Interview Answer

Agile is a software development methodology in which we divide a project into smaller iterations called sprints.

In my project, we generally work in sprints of one or two weeks. At the beginning of each sprint, we have sprint planning, where the team discusses requirements and assigns tasks.

During development, we participate in daily stand-up meetings to discuss completed work, plans for the day, and any blockers.

Once development is completed, we perform testing, demonstrate the features during the sprint review, and discuss improvements during the sprint retrospective.

Agile helps us deliver features incrementally, handle changing requirements, and get regular feedback.
